# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: January 19, 2026**

## Introduction

FaceIt ("we", "our", or "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

## Information We Collect

### Location Data

FaceIt requires access to your device's location to calculate the direction (bearing) to your target destination. This data is:

- **Used locally**: Your location is processed entirely on your device to calculate bearings
- **Not transmitted**: Your precise location is never sent to our servers
- **Temporarily cached**: Location may be cached locally for a short period to improve app performance

### Search History

When you search for locations, the app stores:

- **Search queries**: The names or addresses of locations you search for
- **Last 10 searches**: Only your 10 most recent searches are retained
- **Stored locally**: All search history is stored on your device only

### Favorite Locations

You may choose to save favorite locations. This data includes:

- Location names and coordinates
- Custom labels you assign
- All favorites are stored locally on your device

### Device Sensors

FaceIt accesses the following sensors to provide compass functionality:

- **Magnetometer**: To determine compass heading
- **Accelerometer**: To detect device orientation
- **Gyroscope**: To improve heading accuracy

Sensor data is processed in real-time on your device and is never stored or transmitted.

## Third-Party Services

### Google Maps API

FaceIt uses the Google Maps Geocoding API to convert location names into coordinates. When you search:

- Your search query is sent to Google's servers
- Google returns location data (coordinates, address, place name)
- Google's Privacy Policy applies: https://policies.google.com/privacy

### Firebase Services

FaceIt uses Firebase for the following purposes:

**Firebase Analytics**: Collects anonymized usage data including:

- App opens and screen views
- Feature usage (searches, compass views)
- Device type and OS version
- No personally identifiable information is collected

**Firebase Crashlytics**: Collects crash reports including:

- Device model and OS version
- App state at time of crash
- Anonymous user identifier (not linked to personal identity)

**Firebase Remote Config**: Delivers app configuration updates including:

- Popular location lists
- Feature flags

For Firebase's data practices, see: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

## How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to:

1. **Provide core functionality**: Calculate bearings and distances to locations
2. **Improve the app**: Analyze anonymized usage patterns to fix bugs and add features
3. **Personalize your experience**: Display your recent searches and favorites

## Data Storage and Security

- **Local-first approach**: All personal data (history, favorites, preferences) is stored locally on your device
- **No cloud sync**: We do not store your personal data on external servers
- **No accounts**: FaceIt does not require user accounts or registration
- **Encryption**: Data at rest follows your device's security settings

## Your Rights and Choices

### Access and Control

You have full control over your data:

- **Clear search history**: Settings > Data > Clear Search History
- **Remove favorites**: Swipe to delete individual favorites
- **Reset preferences**: Uninstall and reinstall the app

### Location Permission

You can revoke location permission at any time through your device settings. Without location access, the app will prompt you to enter coordinates manually.

### Opt-Out of Analytics

Analytics collection follows your device's privacy settings:

- iOS: Settings > Privacy > Analytics
- Android: Settings > Google > Ads

### Delete All Data

Uninstalling FaceIt removes all locally stored data including search history, favorites, and preferences.

## Children's Privacy

FaceIt is suitable for users of all ages (rated 4+ / Everyone). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The app does not require any personal information to function.

## International Users

FaceIt is available worldwide. Your data is processed locally on your device regardless of your location. Search queries sent to Google Maps are processed according to Google's global data policies.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected in the "Last Updated" date above. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

## Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or our data practices, please contact us:

- **Email**: support@faceit.app

## Summary

**What we collect**: Search queries, locally cached location, anonymized analytics
**What we don't collect**: Personal information, precise location history, user accounts
**Where data is stored**: On your device (local-first)
**Third parties**: Google Maps (search), Firebase (analytics, crash reports)
**Your control**: Clear history anytime, uninstall removes all data
